About Us

A Story of
Craft & Innovation

Founded by Sandeep Singh and Aditya Narayan Singh, Metadex Business Hub Private Limited was established with a shared vision — to bring innovation and craftsmanship to the furniture industry.

Sandeep's decade of hands-on experience with India's top furniture companies, combined with Aditya's background in business strategy and operations, laid the foundation for a company built on quality, sustainability, and customer-first thinking.

Metadex redefines the Indian furniture market by blending traditional craftsmanship with modern technology — producing stylish, durable furniture for residential, commercial, and institutional spaces.
