About Us

A Story of Craft & Innovation

Founded by Sandeep Singh and Aditya Narayan Singh, Metadex Business Hub Private Limited was established with a shared vision to bring innovation and craftsmanship to the furniture industry.

Metadex aims to redefine the Indian furniture market by blending traditional craftsmanship with modern technology. Committed to producing stylish, durable furniture for residential and commercial spaces — delivering exceptional value with a focus on innovation and ethical practices.
